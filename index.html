Original text

NOTICE (SAMPLE) — Sample Learning Grant
Applicants must submit Form A with proof of age and proof of enrolment by 30 November 2099.
Late forms will not be considered. Verification will take place in the first week of December 2099.
Incomplete forms will be returned.

In plain language

You must send Form A with proof of your age and proof that you are enrolled. The last day is 30 November 2099. Check happens in the first week of December. If anything is missing, your form comes back.

Key dates

  • Deadline: 30 Nov 2099
  • Verification: 1st week of Dec 2099
